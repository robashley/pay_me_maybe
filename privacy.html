Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At Maybe Pay Me, we believe getting paid shouldn't cost you your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our tools.

1. The "No Data Storage" Promise

Most of our tools (like the Late Fee and Due Date Calculators) are "client-side." This means the numbers you type in (invoice amounts, dates, project names) are processed right in your browser.

  • We do not see, collect, or store the specific financial data you enter into our calculators.
  • Once you close your browser tab, that data is gone from our end.

2. Information We Do Collect

We keep things minimal. We only collect information in the following ways:

  • Feedback & Contact: If you use our feedback form, we collect your email address and any message you send so we can respond to you.
  • Usage Analytics: We may use small, privacy-friendly analytics tools to see which tools are popular (e.g., how many people clicked the "Late Fee" tool). This data is anonymised and does not identify you personally.

3. Cookies

We use cookies only where strictly necessary for the website to function or to remember your preferences (like "Dark Mode"). We do not use invasive tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Legal Basis (UK GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR, we process your information based on Legitimate Interests—specifically, the interest of providing and improving free tools for the creative community and responding to your direct enquiries.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to external resources (like Creative Boom or Creative Lives in Progress). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

6. Your Rights

As a UK user, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you (which is usually just your email if you’ve contacted us). To make a request, please use our feedback link.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as we add new tools. We recommend checking this page occasionally to stay informed.
